Editorial Policy

How ToolWools verifies numbers, handles corrections, and stays independent — the standards behind every tool, score, and guide.

Summary

ToolWools is a free, independent editorial site about GTA 6. This page states how we decide what to publish, how we check the numbers, and how we fix mistakes. Nothing on this site is paid placement, and no one outside ToolWools decides what a tool says.

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Independence

ToolWools is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony, or Microsoft, and no platform, publisher, retailer, or sponsor has any say over our tools, scores, or guides. We do not take payment to recommend a product, a store, or an edition. When we link to a retailer or a store page, the link is there because it is useful to the reader, not because anyone paid for it.

How we verify numbers

Every number on this site comes from a named source. First choice is always a primary source: a Rockstar announcement, an official store page, or a measurement we can reproduce on the released game. Where a number is not yet verifiable — because Rockstar has not published it, or because it depends on hardware we have not tested — it is labelled as an estimate, with a stated error band and the source it rests on. Estimates are checked against the released game as soon as we can verify them, and the page carries a "Last verified" date showing when that check happened. If two sources disagree, we say so on the page instead of picking the one that looks tidier. Our methods are public: read the methodology for how each estimate is made.

What we never publish

We do not invent facts. We do not present a rumour, a leak, or a guess as a confirmed number, and we do not cite unnamed "insiders" or "sources close to" anyone. We do not claim expert review we do not have: no page on this site is presented as checked by a veterinarian, an engineer, or any other professional unless that person is named on the page. We do not copy another site's numbers without checking them, and we do not let a tool's output pretend to be more precise than its inputs. A score is a judgement about terms and trade-offs, not a measurement, and it is labelled as one.

Corrections

When a number is wrong, we fix it and say so. Corrections are dated on the affected page, and significant changes are recorded in the page's changelog. If you spot an error, report it to corrections@toolwools.com with the page and the number: we will reproduce the problem, fix it, and date the fix. We do not silently rewrite a number after it has been published.

Tools, scores and estimates

Our calculators and planners estimate, they do not promise. A frame-rate estimate is a range with a stated error band, not a guarantee of what your hardware will do. An edition score compares what each edition adds and what it costs; it is not a recommendation to buy. A tracker shows what we can check, and says plainly what it cannot. If a tool cannot be checked, it stays marked as coming soon rather than shipping with made-up data. Read the disclaimer for the full statement.

Contact

Questions about these standards, or about a specific number on the site, go to jasperbrave001@gmail.com or through the contact page. We read every report, and we answer the ones that change a number.
