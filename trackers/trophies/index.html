Trophy tracker: all 55 trophies & achievements

55 trophies on PlayStation 5 and 55 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox Series X|S, the same list on both consoles: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. There are no online trophies; everything is earned in story mode.

Not the official list, read this first

Neither Rockstar, Sony nor Microsoft has published the trophy list officially. This page is a community compilation: the full list surfaced on 18 Nov 2026, the day before launch, and is carried identically by PowerPyx, GTABase (with Gamerscore values, via GamesRadar+), Insider Gaming and Beebom. The trophy names and tiers match across all four; descriptions are the outlets’ wording of the in-game requirements. If an official list is published and differs, this page will be corrected to match it.

PowerPyx estimates the road to Platinum at around 200 hours and rates the difficulty 4/10, and says no trophy can be missed. 28 of the 55 are hidden trophies tied to the story, they sit behind a spoiler warning below, closed by default.
