Tracker questions, answered

When it opens, what it saves, and what a tracker would cover.

  • Is this tracker live yet?

    Not yet. The tracker is in development and about 40% complete. This page describes what it will track; the interactive checklist is not open yet and will open once development is complete.

  • Will my progress save across devices?

    That is the plan: saved progress will live in your browser by default, with an optional sync once the tracker ships. No account will ever be required.

  • Is there a 100% completion tracker?

    No. There is no completion tracker yet. This page describes the six categories a tracker would cover: story missions, strangers and side jobs, collectibles, trophies, vehicles and weapons. The story missions are confirmed at 91 across 5 chapters in the shipped game.

  • Can I use this now that GTA 6 is out?

    You can read the category breakdowns now. The interactive checklist is not open yet. The tracker is in development and about 40% complete, and this page describes what it will track. The checklist will open once development is complete.

  • Will it cover both consoles?

    Yes. The tracker is built for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with platform-specific trophy and achievement lists.
