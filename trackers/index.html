What changed on this page

  • The old page advertised six trackers that did not exist, and all six tiles pointed at this same page. Each tile above now links to its own page.
  • The old “about 40% complete” and “in development” wording is gone: the four trackers above are open and checkable.
  • Vehicle Garage and Weapon Loadout have no tile and no page, they are not approved. Vehicles and weapons are covered by the vehicle comparison and weapon comparison tools instead.
  • Strangers & side jobs are part of the mission and trophy trackers (8 stranger strands for the Strangers & Freaks trophy), not a separate tile.

How your progress is saved

Ticking a box saves it in this browser (localStorage, one key per tracker: tw-tracker-missions, tw-tracker-collectibles, tw-tracker-100-percent, tw-tracker-trophies). Nothing leaves your device. If browser storage is unavailable, progress lasts for the session only, the tracker page says so plainly.
