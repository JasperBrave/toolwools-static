100% means this, the definition, published

The checklist lives in the game under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and only becomes visible after the story; progress made during the story already counts. Finishing it earns the Gold trophy A Long Road.

The 35 lines fall into three groups, Story & Tasks (12 lines, 45%), Exploration & Activities (20 lines, 45%), Discovery (3 lines, 10%). The percentages are GTABase’s section shares. Nothing on the checklist is missable: everything can still be done in free roam after the story.

100% is not the same as finding everything. Most lines ask for only part of a category (300 of 397 stunts, 50 of 91 stick-ups, 200 of 277 vehicles). PowerPyx puts the checklist at roughly 60% of true game completion. The majority of side quests and strangers are not needed at all.

What this checklist does not cover

  • Most stranger / side-quest strands, only 8 are needed for the Strangers & Freaks trophy, and the 100% list itself does not require them beyond the 100 optional tasks.
  • Full collectible sets, most types are only partially required (15 of 18 card sets, 5 of 7 Kenton sets, 100 of 125 flamingos). Finding every item in a category is beyond 100%.
  • Garages and businesses, owning all 8 garages earns the Little Boxes trophy, but garages are not a line on the 100% checklist. No business can be bought in story mode.
  • Cheats (61 exist) and the full 55-trophy list, the checklist earns one trophy, A Long Road; the other 54 are tracked on the trophy tracker.

Sources, 100% definition

  • PowerPyx, “GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist & Guide”, all 35 lines transcribed from the in-game Progress > Stats > 100% Completion checklist. https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/
  • gamemapa, 18 Nov 2026, same 35 lines; section shares Story & Tasks 45% / Exploration & Activities 45% / Discovery 10%; notes PowerPyx and GTABase list the same requirements line for line. https://gamemapa.com/en/gta-6/guides/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist/
  • MapDex, “GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist: 35 Requirements”, 35 lines in the same three groups; compares Press Start, PowerPyx and Push Square and flags one required figure as disputed (Race Challenge Gold Medals, 10 of 13), marked as such above. https://mapdex.io/gta-6/guides/100-percent-completion-explained
  • No Rockstar documentation of the checklist has been found; the definition above is a transcription of the in-game checklist by post-launch guides, not an official Rockstar publication. All sources accessed 24 Nov 2026.
