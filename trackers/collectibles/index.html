Collectibles tracker: 8 types across Leonida

Eight collectible and activity types, with the count band each one is expected in. Bands are shown as ranges, never a false precise total, the pre-launch bands come from ToolWools’ own map data, and where the released game has since published a real total, the page says so.

Post-launch totals the released game actually counts

The in-game 100% checklist (PowerPyx transcription) counts real totals that supersede some pre-launch bands: 130 stunt jumps (of 397 stunts), 40 unique races, 33 underwater discoveries, 18 collectible card sets (480 cards), 7 Kenton sets, 125 cocaine flamingos, 34 magazines, books & comics, 18 meditation spots, 100 WAiNK locations. Those totals are tracked line by line on the 100% checklist. This page keeps the pre-launch bands for the eight types below, because no per-location list has been published for them yet, a band is honest, an invented exact total is not.

Stunt jumps

Count band: 50–70 · Community-inferred

Enter a number from 0 to 70. Progress is shown against the band, never an invented exact total.

Series precedent: GTA V shipped 50 unique stunt jumps; San Andreas shipped 70. Every 3D-era GTA has included them.

GTA 6 signal: No stunt-jump prompt has been shown. Trailer footage includes ramped causeway approaches and elevated roadworks that fit the pattern.

Post-launch check: the released game's 100% checklist counts 130 stunt jumps (of 397 stunts). The pre-launch band was 50–70.

Likely regions: Vice City, Grassrivers, Mount Kalaga National Park (see the interactive map)

Hidden packages / caches

Count band: 50–100 · Community-inferred

Enter a number from 0 to 100. Progress is shown against the band, never an invented exact total.

Series precedent: Vice City (2002) had 100 hidden packages; GTA Online reused the format as buried caches across land and seabed.

GTA 6 signal: Not shown. A keys-and-wetland map with a diving layer is the strongest possible fit for the format, which is why it is listed, as an expectation only.

Post-launch check: no published total for hidden packages yet. The band stands until a per-location list is published.

Likely regions: Leonida Keys, Offshore reef & open water (see the interactive map)

Wildlife sightings

Not countable, a list to spot, not a checklist with a false denominator. · Strongly implied

Series precedent: RDR2 shipped 178 animal species with a compendium; GTA V limited wildlife to a small set outside the city.

GTA 6 signal: Alligators, wading birds and marine life appear in official wetland and water footage, which is a substantially richer showing than GTA V had at reveal.

Post-launch check: the 100% checklist asks for 100 of 255 animals studied, wildlife is a real system, but there is no fixed sighting list to check off.

Likely regions: Grassrivers, Mount Kalaga National Park, Leonida Keys (see the interactive map)

Dive sites and wrecks

Count band: 20–40 · Strongly implied

Enter a number from 0 to 40. Progress is shown against the band, never an invented exact total.

Series precedent: GTA V modelled a full ocean floor with wrecks, submarine parts and 30 nuclear waste canisters.

GTA 6 signal: Underwater and open-water footage appears in official trailers. The reef tract is the single most obvious use of a Florida setting.

Post-launch check: the 100% checklist counts 33 underwater discoveries (shipwrecks, part of Kenton's questline), close to this band, but a different count, so the band is kept.

Likely regions: Offshore reef & open water, Leonida Keys (see the interactive map)

Street races

Count band: 15–30 · Community-inferred

Enter a number from 0 to 30. Progress is shown against the band, never an invented exact total.

Series precedent: GTA V shipped street, sea and off-road race series unlocked through the world rather than a menu.

GTA 6 signal: Not confirmed as a standalone activity. Trailer footage shows a car-culture scene, modified vehicles, night meets, consistent with race content.

Post-launch check: the 100% checklist counts 40 unique races (30 wins required) and 13 race-challenge gold medals. The band was written before those totals were known.

Likely regions: Vice City, Ambrosia (see the interactive map)

Purchasable properties

Count band: 10–25 · Community-inferred

Enter a number from 0 to 25. Progress is shown against the band, never an invented exact total.

Series precedent: Vice City (2002) built its whole progression loop on property purchase; GTA V reduced it to a handful of businesses.

GTA 6 signal: Not confirmed. Rockstar's Vice City return and the two-protagonist structure make an ownership loop plausible, but no property UI has been shown.

Post-launch check: the only purchasable property confirmed so far is garages, 8 for sale, $160,000 total (ToolWools business tracker, 21 Nov 2026). No other property purchase is confirmed.

Likely regions: Vice City, Port Gellhorn (see the interactive map)

Landmark viewpoints

Not countable, a list to spot, not a checklist with a false denominator. · Strongly implied

Series precedent: RDR2 used points of interest and vistas; GTA V used the tourist-photo mechanic sparingly.

GTA 6 signal: Official footage leans heavily on scenic framing, beach strip, skyline, wetland sunsets, which usually accompanies some form of photo or discovery system.

Post-launch check: the 100% checklist asks for 100 WAiNK locations scanned (landmarks and sculptures), a real discovery system, but with no fixed viewpoint list to check off.

Likely regions: Vice City, Mount Kalaga National Park (see the interactive map)

In-world social feed moments

Not countable, a list to spot, not a checklist with a false denominator. · Strongly implied

Series precedent: New to the series. GTA V had Lifeinvader as set dressing rather than a systemic feature.

GTA 6 signal: Trailer 1 is framed as vertical-video clips from an in-world feed, and Rockstar's own reveal materials lean on that device, the clearest new-system signal in any official material.

Post-launch check: no published list of feed moments exists yet. This stays a list to spot, not a checklist.

Likely regions: Vice City (see the interactive map)
