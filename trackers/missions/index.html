Mission tracker: all 91 story missions

The full story, chapter by chapter. 91 main missions across 5 chapters, 22 in Just Like Paradise, 11 in Paradise Lost, 26 in Tits Ass Drug Money, 25 in Vice City, Baby, 7 in Top of the World, Ma. Rockstar has published no official mission count; the 91 figure is corroborated by five independent post-launch guides (see sources below). Mission names are listed, that is the tracker’s purpose, but no plot details are given beyond the short activity line each guide publishes.

Confidence:ConfirmedStrongly impliedCommunity-inferredConfirmed = named or shown in official Rockstar material. Strongly implied = strongly implied by official material or corroborated by several independent guides. Community-inferred = guide/community transcription, treat as an estimate.

How the 91 is counted

A mission counts if it has its own name in the game’s story list. Two optional missions in Chapter 2 (Thrillbillies; Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab) and one optional job in Chapter 3 (Kitty Litter Labs) are not part of the 91, lists that include them reach 93 or more. They are listed separately below, outside the 91 total.
