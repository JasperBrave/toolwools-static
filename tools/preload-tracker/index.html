What this page is, and what it is not

A record and a watchlist, not a feed. Every figure on this page belongs to exactly one of three data classes, and the three are never mixed in one table.

  • Verified

    Rockstar Newswire statements and the game's own release record, always with the source and date beside the figure.

  • Reported, not verified

    Guide and rollout reporting (sizes, rollout waves), each with its source and date. Repetition by aggregators is not independent confirmation.

  • Unknown

    The contents of the two pre-launch updates; whether or when any further patch, DLC, online mode or PC version exists. Unknown is stated, never filled in.

What 01.002.002 fixes (official notes, Rockstar Support, 23 Nov 2026)

  • Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly.
  • Fixed several issues that resulted in phone calls not triggering correctly, including those that progressed the story or unlocked mission content.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the players being unable to lose their Wanted Level.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in partners not handing over bags so that players could sell them to fences.

The notes do not mention the infinite money glitch or the PS5 crash error CE-108255-1, their silence does not confirm either is fixed. The contents of 1.002.000 and 1.002.001 remain unknown: Rockstar published no notes for them, and datamined claims about what they changed are unverified.

GTA 6 preload and update questions

The preload that happened, the updates that landed, and what is next.

  • Is there a GTA 6 preload now?

    No. The launch preload ran 10–19 Nov 2026 and is over. GTA 6 launched 19 Nov 2026; new buyers download the full 212 GB install at purchase. There is no next preload on any announced horizon, the next preload would only exist if Rockstar announces a PC version, which it has not.

  • What was the GTA 6 preload date?

    PlayStation 5: midnight local time, Tue 10 Nov 2026. Xbox Series X|S: a single global unlock at 12:00 AM GMT, Tue 10 Nov 2026. Code-in-a-box: from Thu 12 Nov 2026. The install was up to 212 GB, for digital pre-orders (Standard or Ultimate).

  • What is the latest GTA 6 update?

    01.002.002, released 23 Nov 2026, the first post-launch patch (PS5 ~603.8 MB, Xbox ~1.6 GB), and the first update with official patch notes. Before it: 1.002.001 (16 Nov) and 1.002.000 (13 Nov), both pre-launch, both without notes. Last checked 23 Nov 2026.

  • Are there GTA 6 patch notes?

    Yes, for 01.002.002 only. Rockstar published official notes for the 23 Nov 2026 update (crash fixes, weather, autosave, phone calls, Wanted Level, Gym deadlift bar, fence bag handover). The two pre-launch updates have no notes, and their contents remain unknown.

  • Is there GTA 6 DLC?

    No. Rockstar has announced no story DLC, expansion, online mode or PC version, and no dates for any of them. The only dated post-launch item is a music album (Original Score by Justice, 11 Dec 2026), not game content. This page is updated the day Rockstar announces one.

  • When is the next GTA 6 update?

    Unknown. No date has been announced. This page is the watchlist: the schedule section above stays empty with a current “last checked” date until Rockstar announces a dated patch or DLC.

  • How big is the GTA 6 download now?

    The base install is up to 212 GB, plus the three title updates so far (about 1.07 GB + 602.5 MB + 603.8 MB on PS5). The download-time calculator works out how long that takes on your connection; the storage planner works out the space.

  • Can I get reminded about the next update?

    Not yet. No patch or DLC reminder exists, the calendar tool holds the launch reminder only. The day Rockstar announces a dated patch or DLC, its reminder is wired into the calendar tool, and this page gains the row. The newsletter announces tool launches.
