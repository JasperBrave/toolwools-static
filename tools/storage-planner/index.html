How the storage total is calculated

The total starts with the base install plus the day-one patch plus planned DLC. A safety buffer is then added as a percentage of that subtotal. The platform table subtracts the total from each console's usable space — the space left after the operating system and reserved partitions.

subtotal = base + patch + dlc
headroom = subtotal × (bufferPct / 100)
total = subtotal + headroom
remaining = usableSpace − total
PS5 667 GB · PS5 Slim 842 GB · Xbox Series X 802 GB · Xbox Series S 364 GB
  • Install size is up to 212 GB (base) plus a 1.7–3.8 GB day-one patch.
  • Usable space is what is left after the OS and system reserves: PS5 667 GB of 825 GB, PS5 Slim 842 GB of 1 TB, Xbox Series X 802 GB of 1 TB, Xbox Series S 364 GB of 512 GB.
  • The 20% buffer keeps headroom for patches to unpack and stops SSDs running nearly full.
  • DLC size is a planning estimate for the first year, not a Rockstar announcement.

Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to the questions people actually ask about GTA 6 storage now that it has launched.

  • Plan on 258–294 GB free. That's a 212 GB base install, a 2.8 GB day-one patch, roughly 30 GB of first-year DLC, plus a 20% headroom buffer so future updates don't stall. On console the console reserves an additional 60–80 GB of system space you can't reclaim.

  • Rockstar has not confirmed, but console builds are typically 5–10% smaller than PC because they ship a single hardware target. Expect ~140 GB on PS5 / Xbox Series X. Both consoles reserve some raw NAND for the OS: PS5 gives you 667 GB of the 825 GB SSD, Series X gives 802 GB of 1 TB.

  • Yes. Rockstar's recent launches have shipped 10–20 GB day-one patches — RDR2 needed one within hours of launch. Reserve 2.8 GB by default; the slider lets you push it to 100 GB if you want maximum headroom.

  • Using GTA V Online as the precedent, Rockstar shipped roughly 15 GB of new content per year for a decade. This tool defaults DLC to 30 GB (year one), but you can dial it up to 200 GB if you plan to stay through several content seasons.

  • It's tight. Between Windows (~40 GB), typical utility apps (~30 GB), one or two other AAA games (~200 GB) and GTA 6 (~200 GB), a 500 GB SSD fills up fast. Plan a 1 TB NVMe as the practical launch target — prices are around $60 as of 2026.

  • USB 3.2 external SSDs work but cap around 1000 MB/s; a good internal NVMe delivers 5000-7000 MB/s. Streaming stalls in open-world play show up on slower drives. Prefer internal NVMe when you have a slot; external SSD beats an HDD by a wide margin.

  • Games can't patch in place — they need enough free space to download the new files, verify them, and swap them in. Without ~20% headroom, mid-cycle patches fail with 'insufficient space' errors even when the visible install size is small.

  • GTA V Legacy is ~110 GB and GTA V Enhanced (2025) is ~105 GB after remastering. GTA 6's fidelity leap — full ray tracing, dense open world with denser NPC systems — puts the install at up to 212 GB before day-one patches.
