How this tracker works

Three parts: the launch instant, the milestone list, and your timezone.

  1. 01

    The launch instant is the anchor

    The launch instant was Nov 19, 2026 at 05:00 UTC, the shared launch moment. The countdown ran to that instant and stopped at launch — the time shown above is the launch moment in your timezone.

  2. 02

    Milestones carry their status with them

    Each milestone shows its date and its status. The Nov 19, 2026 launch is the confirmed anchor. Preload and the first post-launch patch (01.002.002, Nov 23, 2026) are complete; no online mode has been announced.

  3. 03

    Pick a timezone to see your launch moment

    Choose your city and the launch instant is shown in local time. The choice is stored in the page link, so a shared link opens with the same timezone already selected.
