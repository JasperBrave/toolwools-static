How to read the GTA 6 GPU tier list

The table is the tool, filters narrow it, rows deep-link out.

  1. 01

    Pick a resolution

    1080p, 1440p or 4K. The table swaps its FPS column to that resolution's Ultra-native estimate so you compare cards on the target you actually play at.

  2. 02

    Filter by vendor, VRAM or budget

    Narrow to NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, set a minimum VRAM floor (8 / 12 / 16 GB) and cap MSRP. The table re-sorts by raw score inside each tier.

  3. 03

    Compare tiers S through F

    Each row shows tier (S–F), 1080p / 1440p / 4K Ultra FPS estimates and the one-line verdict, the same copy that appears on the FPS calculator's output panel.

  4. 04

    Open any card in the FPS calculator

    Click a row to open the full FPS calculator with that GPU pre-selected. Add your CPU and preset to see 1% lows, DLSS deltas and the exact bottleneck limiter.

How each GPU is scored and slotted into a tier

Deterministic score. Perf part comes from the shared FPS/bottleneck engine (RTX 4090 = 100). VRAM at 0.8× GB rewards the 12 GB+ cards that hold up at 1440p Ultra with RT on. RT capability adds a flat +6. An age penalty of −1.2 per year past release keeps the ranking honest, an 8-year-old GTX 1070 shouldn't sit next to a same-perf modern card.

raw = perf + vram × 0.8 + (rtCapable ? 6 : 0) − max(0, 2026 − releaseYear) × 1.2

tier =
  raw ≥ 110 → S
  raw ≥ 75  → A
  raw ≥ 55  → B
  raw ≥ 40  → C
  raw ≥ 25  → D
  else       → F
  • Perf part is the same 0–118 score used by the FPS and bottleneck calculators, RTX 4090 = 100, RTX 5090 = 118, calibrated to Digital Foundry's RDR2 launch and Cyberpunk 2077 patch 2.0 reviews.
  • VRAM weight (0.8× GB) was tuned so 12 GB cards outrank same-perf 8 GB cards by ~3 points, enough to matter, not enough to override a full perf tier.
  • RT bonus is a flat +6 rather than proportional because Rockstar's engine (RAGE 9) exposes RT as a preset toggle, not a per-effect slider.
  • Age penalty caps at 12 points (10-year-old cards); anything older is already in F tier on raw perf alone.
  • Anchor calibration: RTX 4090 → S, RTX 4070 → A, RTX 4060 → B, RTX 3060 → C, GTX 1660 Super → D, GTX 1060 → F. Any threshold change that breaks an anchor is rejected.

Assumptions Perf score reuses the FPS engine (RTX 4090 = 100), measured at 1080p Ultra native with a top CPU so the tier reflects GPU capability, not CPU cap. Best GPU for GTA 6: buyer questions

Card-by-card answers, tied to the scores above.

  • What is the best GPU for GTA 6?

    For no-compromise 4K Ultra with ray tracing the RTX 5090 leads the S tier, followed by the RTX 4090 and RTX 5080. At 1440p, the RTX 5070 Ti and RX 7900 XTX offer 74–78% of flagship performance at roughly half the price. The 'best' GPU is the highest-tier card that fits your resolution and budget, filter this list to see it.

  • Can an RTX 4060 run GTA 6?

    Yes, the RTX 4060 lands in B tier. It handles 1080p Ultra at ~60 fps native and 1440p High with DLSS Quality comfortably. The 8 GB VRAM buffer is the only real risk: at 1440p Ultra with RT and HD textures on, expect stutter. For a GTA 6 build the RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB or the step-up RTX 5070 is the safer pick.

  • How is the tier list calculated?

    Every GPU gets a raw score: perf (from the FPS engine, RTX 4090 = 100) + VRAM × 0.8 + a flat +6 if the card supports hardware ray tracing − 1.2 for each year since its release. Tier bands are S ≥ 110, A ≥ 75, B ≥ 55, C ≥ 40, D ≥ 25, F below. The formula is deterministic and versioned, re-runs match to the point.

  • Why does age matter for GTA 6 GPU ranking?

    GTA 6 shipped with driver optimisations aimed at current architectures (Ada, Blackwell, RDNA 3/4). Older cards see slower driver improvements and often miss out on the newest upscalers, a Pascal GTX 1070 can't run DLSS at all. The age penalty (−1.2 per year) reflects that gap; it drops an 8-year-old card ~10 points below a same-perf modern card.

  • Is 8 GB VRAM enough for GTA 6?

    For 1080p High / Very High, yes. For 1440p Ultra or 4K with HD textures and ray tracing, no. VRAM allocation for modern open-world games at those settings sits at 10–14 GB. The tier list rewards 12 GB+ cards precisely because that's the launch-day comfort zone. See the VRAM calculator for exact card-vs-need deltas.

  • Should I wait for the RTX 50 Super refresh?

    If you already own a B or A tier card (RTX 4060 or better) the answer is 'wait and see', GTA 6 will land on current hardware fine and Super refreshes historically bump raster performance 10–15% at similar prices. On C tier or below (RTX 3060 or older), the upgrade case is stronger than the wait case.

  • How does the tier list handle AMD cards vs NVIDIA?

    Raw perf is normalised to the same 0–118 scale (RTX 4090 = 100, RTX 5090 = 118, RX 7900 XTX ≈ 92). RT capability adds a flat +6 for any hardware-RT card, NVIDIA and AMD both qualify. AMD tends to price more aggressively per raster tier, so filtering by budget usually surfaces a Radeon at the top.

  • Where do laptop / mobile GPUs sit?

    Mobile SKUs are ~20–30% slower than their desktop namesake at matched TDP. The list is desktop-only; a laptop RTX 4070 usually maps to roughly a desktop RTX 4060 Ti / 4060 in tier. See the FPS calculator for a manual mobile estimate, pick the desktop card one tier down.

  • What's the minimum GPU I can get away with for GTA 6?

    The tier list treats C as the practical floor, RTX 3060, RX 6600, GTX 1660 Super with DLSS/FSR at 1080p Low/Medium. D tier cards (GTX 1660, RX 5600 XT) will boot but only reach about 61 fps at 1080p Low, and fall to 30 fps at 1080p High. F tier (GTX 1060 / 1050 Ti class) misses even Rockstar's likely minimum spec.

  • Does the tier list account for ray tracing?

    Yes, RT capability gives every eligible card a flat +6 in the raw score. Actual RT-on FPS is not the ranking axis (the score uses raster to keep the reference stable); jump to the FPS calculator to compare RT-on and RT-off numbers on the same card.

GPU spec terms used in the tier table

Tier
Letter grade S → F derived from the raw score. S is flagship 4K, A is enthusiast 1440p Ultra, B is 1080p Ultra / 1440p High sweet spot, C is 1080p High, D is below launch floor, F is not viable.
Raw score
Perf + VRAM × 0.8 + RT bonus − age penalty. Deterministic, inputs are the FPS engine perf number, card VRAM in GB, RT support flag and release year.
Age penalty
−1.2 for every calendar year since the card's release. Compensates for slower driver updates and missing upscaler support on older architectures.
MSRP
Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price at launch, in USD. Used for the budget filter, street prices can differ.
