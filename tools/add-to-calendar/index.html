How the calendar event is built

Every calendar button generates the exact same underlying event: DTSTART 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z, 60-minute duration, two VALARM reminders (-P1D and -PT1H). Google/Outlook/Yahoo use the vendor's URL scheme; Apple and Windows Calendar receive a signed RFC 5545 .ics payload. The launch anchor is a single constant — it flips to the official Rockstar time the instant it's published.

DTSTART = 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z   (00:00 America/New_York, EST UTC-5)
DTEND   = DTSTART + 60min
VALARM  = TRIGGER -P1D   (24h before)
VALARM  = TRIGGER -PT1H  (1h before)
  • Launch anchor at 2026-11-19 00:00 America/New_York (05:00 UTC) matches Rockstar's GTA V (Sep 17, 2013) and RDR2 (Oct 26, 2018) release-day unlock schedules.
  • 60-minute event duration is arbitrary; calendars treat the DTSTART as the alarm trigger — end time is only shown as a block on day view.
  • Two VALARM reminders (24h and 1h) are the industry-standard reminder cadence — long enough for pre-load prep, short enough to catch the launch.
  • Timezone offsets are hard-coded for Nov 19, 2026: Northern hemisphere DST is off (EST, GMT, CET, JST unchanged); Southern hemisphere DST is on (AEDT, NZDT).

GTA 6 release date reminder questions

Timezones, .ics files and what happens if the date slips.

  • When is the GTA 6 release date?

    November 19, 2026. Rockstar confirmed the date in the second official trailer and reiterated it in the May 2026 investor update. Historically Rockstar unlocks at midnight local time in each region on the release date.

  • What time does GTA 6 unlock in my timezone?

    This tool anchors launch at 2026-11-19 00:00 America/New_York (05:00 UTC), matching Rockstar's GTA V and RDR2 precedent. The unlock table lists local times for 14 major cities. It is anchored to the confirmed unlock schedule.

  • How do I add GTA 6 to Google Calendar?

    Click the Google button. It opens Google Calendar with the event pre-filled — title, description, location and duration. Confirm and it's on your calendar with two reminders baked in (24 hours and 1 hour before launch).

  • Does this work on iPhone / Apple Calendar?

    Yes. The Apple button downloads an .ics file. iOS Safari opens it directly in Calendar; on macOS it opens in Calendar.app. Both platforms honour the two VALARM reminders.

  • Has the unlock time been confirmed?

    Yes — it already has. The launch anchor is a single constant in the tool, now set to the confirmed unlock time: Nov 19, 2026, 00:00 America/New_York (05:00 UTC). Every calendar button and the countdown use that confirmed value.

  • Can I get a reminder for the GTA 6 pre-load?

    The generated event includes two reminders: 24 hours before and 1 hour before launch. Rockstar typically opens pre-load 48–72 hours before launch — we'll add a separate pre-load event once the exact window is announced.

  • Is the GTA 6 release date confirmed?

    Yes. Take-Two Interactive confirmed Nov 19, 2026 in the May 2026 earnings call and Rockstar's Trailer 2 uses the same date. Any slip would be publicly announced through the same channels — check /trackers for the live status.

  • What time does GTA 6 unlock in the UK and Europe?

    On the midnight-ET precedent, launch lands at 05:00 GMT in London, 06:00 CET in Berlin, Paris and Madrid, and 07:00 EET in Helsinki. That puts UK and EU players in front of a workday morning unlock rather than a midnight one — book the day off accordingly.

  • Do console and PC unlock at the same time?

    GTA 6 ships on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026; the PC version has no announced date. On console, PlayStation and Xbox both honour the publisher's regional unlock, so the two storefronts flip within the same minute.

  • How big is the pre-load and when should I start it?

    Rockstar opens pre-load 48–72 hours out and RDR2 shipped at 99 GB, so budget 212 GB or more. Start the moment it opens: launch-week CDN congestion routinely doubles download times. Our storage planner sizes the free space you need.

  • What happens to my reminder if the date slips?

    The event you exported is a static .ics file — it will not move on its own. If Rockstar changes the date we update the anchor here, so re-export and delete the old entry. The trackers page logs every official date statement with its source.

  • Can I add the reminder on Android without Google Calendar?

    Yes. Download the .ics file and open it with any calendar app that supports the iCalendar standard — Samsung Calendar, Outlook, Proton Calendar and Fossify all import it, including both alarms.

  • Why does the tool default to midnight ET?

    Rockstar unlocked GTA V (2013), RDR2 (2018) and GTA Online updates at 00:00 America/New_York. Until Rockstar publishes a different schedule, ET midnight is the most defensible default. Adjust with the timezone selector for your local time.

Calendar and timezone terms used on this page

.ics file
RFC 5545 calendar interchange format. Downloadable file that any Apple, Windows or Linux calendar app can import.
VALARM
The reminder inside a calendar event. This tool adds two: 24 hours and 1 hour before launch.
Pre-load
The window before launch when digital storefronts open the download so the game is playable at 00:00.
Unlock time
The moment the game becomes playable. Rockstar traditionally unlocks at 00:00 local time in each region.
