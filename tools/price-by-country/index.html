How each country's price is calculated from the $79.99 anchor

There is one input the world agrees on, the US Standard price, so that is the only anchor. For a non-regional market the shelf price is the USD anchor multiplied by a dated reference FX rate, rounded to the currency's conventional precision (zero decimals for JPY, KRW, IDR, VND and similar). Tax is then either extracted from the shelf price (tax-inclusive markets) or added on top (US, Canada). The receipt total converts back to USD at the same rate to give a comparable figure, and the difference against the US total drives the price band. For regionally-priced markets the storefront's local price replaces the conversion, and the Ultimate rung scales by the same USD ratio so the whole ladder respects the discount rather than only the base game.

shelfLocal =
  regionallyPriced ? storeLocal × (editionUsd / 79.99)
                   : editionUsd × fxPerUsd

taxLocal =
  taxInclusive ? shelfLocal − shelfLocal / (1 + taxRate)
               : shelfLocal × taxRate

totalLocal = taxInclusive ? shelfLocal : shelfLocal + taxLocal
usdEquivalent = totalLocal / fxPerUsd
vsUs% = (usdEquivalent − usTotal) / usTotal × 100

band =
  vsUs% ≤ −30 → cheapest
  vsUs% ≤ −8  → below average
  vsUs% <  8  → typical
  vsUs% < 30  → above average
  else         → most expensive
  • Only the $79.99 US Standard and $99.99 US Ultimate prices are treated as fact. Every other figure is either a dated conversion or a modelled regional price, and both are labelled as such in the interface, not just in the small print.
  • Regional price levels come from the standing list prices these storefronts have used for $59.99–$79.99 releases in each market, not from a leak or a rumoured listing.
  • FX rates are frozen at 2026-11-20 so results are reproducible; the date renders next to every converted figure.
  • Zero-decimal currencies (JPY, KRW, IDR, VND, CLP, COP, ARS, NGN, PKR, BDT, PHP) round to whole units, matching how storefronts actually list them.
  • US sales tax uses a 7% national average because it is set at state and county level; treat the US receipt total as ±3% rather than exact.
  • There is no Deluxe edition. The Ultimate rung costs $99.99 (confirmed by Rockstar); its bundled GTA$ figure is modelled.

How much does GTA 6 cost? Country and edition questions

Direct answers on price, tax, editions and region switching.

  • How much does GTA 6 cost?

    Rockstar has confirmed $79.99 USD for the Standard edition, with pre-orders opening 25 June 2026. In your own currency the number depends on two things: your storefront's regional price and your country's consumption tax. Pick your country above for the receipt total, for example the modelled figure is ₹4,499 in India, £69.99 in the UK and A$124.95 in Australia.

  • What is the GTA 6 price in India in rupees?

    The modelled Standard price is ₹4,499 including 18% GST, based on India's long-standing regional pricing on Steam, PlayStation Store and Xbox. That is roughly $51 at the reference rate, about 40% below the US receipt total. If Rockstar breaks with regional pricing and converts directly, the figure would land near ₹6,900 plus GST; we will update the page the day the storefronts list.

  • Why is GTA 6 cheaper in some countries?

    Two separate reasons, and they get confused constantly. Regional pricing is a publisher decision to set a lower local price so the game stays affordable relative to local income. That is why India, Brazil, Türkiye and Argentina sit at the bottom of the table. Tax differences are a distant second: a 25% Nordic VAT versus a 5% UAE VAT moves the receipt by far less than a regional price cut does. This page shows both lines so you can tell which one is doing the work.

  • Can I buy GTA 6 from a cheaper country?

    Technically the price gap is real; practically it is a bad trade. Storefront region is tied to your account and, on Steam, PlayStation and Xbox, to a payment method issued in that country. Region-hopping to buy cheaper breaks the terms of service on every major platform, and enforcement ranges from a blocked purchase to a locked library. Treat the Compare tab as a price comparison, not a purchasing route.

  • Is the GTA 6 Ultimate edition price confirmed?

    No. The Standard price ($79.99) and the Ultimate price ($99.99) are official. There is no Deluxe edition. Ultimate's bundled GTA$ figure is modelled, not confirmed, and the changelog will record the day real numbers replace it.

  • Is the Ultimate edition worth it for the GTA$ bundle?

    Divide the premium by the bundled in-game money. The modelled Ultimate rung costs $30 over Standard for 1,000,000 GTA$, which works out at about $3 per 100,000 GTA$, cheaper than any Shark Card rate in GTA Online history. If you intend to play Online seriously, that is the strongest single argument for the upgrade; if you are a story-mode-only player, the bundle is worth nothing to you.

  • Does this page use live exchange rates?

    No, and that is deliberate. Rates are static reference values dated 2026-11-20 and shown on screen. A live feed would mean the price you screenshot today disagrees with the price you quote tomorrow, and every FAQ answer and comparison on the page would drift. For a launch price you are planning around, a dated reference rate is more useful than a ticking one.

  • Does the price include tax?

    It depends on where you are, which is exactly why the tax line is separate. Most markets, the EU, UK, Japan, India, Australia, Brazil, quote tax-inclusive shelf prices, so the tax is already inside the number you see. The US and Canada add tax at checkout, so the shelf price understates what you pay; this page uses a 7% national average for US sales tax because it genuinely varies by state and county.

  • Will GTA 6 cost more on PC than on console?

    Historically no, but the PC version is expected later than the console launch, and delayed PC ports have sometimes launched at the same nominal price with regional prices reset to current FX. The pricing here is platform-agnostic; when the PC release date and storefront listings appear we will add a platform split rather than guess at one now.

  • What happens to these numbers when currencies move?

    Regionally-priced markets barely move. Publishers reset those prices in occasional passes, not weekly. Converted markets move directly with FX, so a 10% currency slide shows up as roughly a 10% higher local price at the next storefront reprice. The reference date on the page tells you how stale the conversion is; anything older than a quarter should be treated as indicative.

  • Is $79.99 a price rise for the series?

    Yes. GTA 5 launched at $59.99 in 2013 and Red Dead Redemption 2 at $59.99 in 2018. Adjusted for US inflation, $59.99 in 2013 is roughly $80 in 2026, so in real terms the Standard edition is close to flat. The sticker is what changed. That framing matters for regional markets, where local incomes have not tracked US inflation and the regional discount does the heavy lifting.

  • Where does the pre-order date fit in?

    Pre-orders open 25 June 2026, which is when real storefront listings replace every modelled number on this page. If you want the reminder rather than the maths, the release date reminder tool writes both the pre-order window and launch day to your calendar in your own timezone.

Pricing and tax terms used on this page

Regional pricing
A publisher setting a deliberately lower local price for a market instead of converting the USD price. Not a discount or a sale, it is the market's standing list price.
Shelf price
The number the storefront displays. Tax-inclusive in most of the world, tax-exclusive in the US and Canada.
Receipt total
What actually leaves your account: shelf price plus any tax added at checkout. The figure this page ranks countries by.
USD equivalent
The receipt total converted back to USD at the dated reference rate. The only way to compare 34 currencies on one axis.
VAT / GST / sales tax
Consumption tax on the purchase. VAT and GST are normally inside the shelf price; US sales tax is added at checkout and varies by state.
Edition premium
What the Ultimate rung costs above Standard, in your currency. Divided by bundled GTA$ it becomes a comparable value rate.
