Where is GTA 6 set?

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida, with Vice City, a stand-in for Miami, on the south-east coast. It is the first mainline return to Vice City since 2002, and the first GTA to cover an entire state rather than a single city and its hinterland.

    Is the leaked GTA 6 map real?

    Short answer: not usefully. The 2022 breach was genuine development footage, but it showed an unfinished build years from release. Almost every "leaked GTA 6 map" image circulating since is a fan reconstruction, an upscale, or a fabrication built on top of it.

    Development geometry is not shipping geometry. Regions get resized, merged and cut late in production, GTA V's own pre-release maps differ visibly from what shipped. Treating a datamined outline as the final map is how most fan wikis end up publishing coastlines that never existed.

    This page reproduces no leaked or datamined imagery. The canvas is original vector artwork, and every region and district resolves to a record with a named source. Where the evidence stops, the entry is labelled Community-inferred rather than quietly presented as fact, set the evidence filter to Confirmed to strip the map back to official material only.

    GTA 6 map, setting and leak questions

    Evidence-graded answers, confirmed, implied or inferred.

    • How big is the GTA 6 map?

      Rockstar has not published a landmass figure, so any exact number you see is an estimate. Based on the six named regions, the state-scale setting and Rockstar's pattern of roughly doubling explorable space each generation, a defensible band is 90–170 km² of playable land, around 1.2× to 2.27× the size of GTA V. Expect the perceived size to be larger than the raw number suggests, since density and interior count matter more than area.

    • Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?

      Almost certainly yes. GTA V's playable land measures about 75 km², and GTA 6 spans an entire fictional state containing a major city, a keys chain, a wetland interior, farmland, a panhandle town and a national park. Even the low end of the estimate band exceeds GTA V. What is not yet knowable is by how much.

    • Where is GTA 6 located?

      In the state of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. The anchor city is Vice City on the south-east coast, standing in for Miami. Other named areas are the Leonida Keys (Florida Keys), Grassrivers (Everglades), Ambrosia (inland farm country), Port Gellhorn (the panhandle) and Mount Kalaga National Park (north Florida forest).

    • Is there an official GTA 6 map?

      No. Rockstar has not released a map image or an in-game map screenshot. Everything circulating online is either a community reconstruction from trailer footage or leaked material. This page uses a hand-built schematic derived from official footage and named regions, and labels every entry with how strongly it is supported.

    • What is the GTA 6 map based on?

      Florida. Vice City maps to Miami, the Leonida Keys to the Florida Keys, Grassrivers to the Everglades, Mount Kalaga to the Ocala forest region, and Port Gellhorn to the panhandle around Panama City. Leonida is a compressed, stylised version of the state rather than a one-to-one recreation.

    • Has the GTA 6 map been confirmed?

      The regions have been named in official Rockstar material; the map's shape, scale and boundaries have not. That is exactly the distinction the evidence filter on this page exists for, switch it to Confirmed to see only officially sourced entries.

    • Does this page use leaked map images?

      No. The canvas is an original vector schematic built from region records with cited sources. Leaked or datamined imagery is deliberately excluded, it is legally risky and, in practice, frequently wrong. Nothing on this page is reproduced from leaked assets.

    • Will GTA 6 have an underwater map area?

      Likely, though it is only implied rather than confirmed. Official trailers include water and shoreline footage, and GTA V shipped with a fully modelled ocean floor. Offshore reef and open water is tagged 'strongly implied' on this map rather than confirmed.

    • How does the GTA 6 map compare to Red Dead Redemption 2?

      RDR2's playable land also measures roughly 75 km², so the comparison is close to the GTA V one, the GTA 6 estimate band lands at about 1.2× to 2.27× RDR2. The meaningful difference is density: Leonida's area is dominated by a dense coastal city, whereas RDR2's is mostly open wilderness.

    • What are the districts of Vice City in GTA 6?

      Official material confirms three: Ocean Drive, the wider Vice Beach island and Downtown. Footage strongly implies the port, the causeway islands, a Cuban commercial quarter, the northern residential blocks, the marina and the wetland edge, none of which Rockstar has named. The industrial south, airport and western suburbs are community reconstructions based on real Miami geography, the district view labels each one so you can tell them apart.

    • Which Vice City district should I explore first in GTA 6?

      On the evidence available, Ocean Drive and Downtown are the two districts Rockstar has shown most, and they are near-opposites: Ocean Drive has the highest crowd and neon density but little height, while Downtown is the only district with real skyline verticality, rooftops and an elevated expressway. The district comparator on this page scores any two districts against each other on built density, verticality, nightlife, water access, expected police heat and visible wealth, so you can see the trade-off rather than take a single recommendation.

    • Is the leaked GTA 6 map real?

      The 2022 leak was genuine stolen development footage, but it showed an unfinished build years from release, and most 'leaked map' images circulating since are fan reconstructions or outright fabrications layered on top of it. Development-build geometry is not shipping geometry: regions get resized, cut and added. This page reproduces no leaked or datamined imagery, every entry is a hand-built schematic traced to an official source.

    • What collectibles will be in the GTA 6 map?

      None have been confirmed. Based on series precedent, expect stunt jumps (50–70 in recent GTAs), hidden packages or caches (50–100), dive sites and wrecks (20–40) and street races (15–30). Wildlife, landmark viewpoints and the in-world social feed are the activities with the strongest signal in official footage. Anyone publishing collectible coordinates today is inventing them.

    • What does the GTA 6 map look like?

      A Florida-shaped state: a dense coastal city in the south-east, a keys chain trailing south-west, a huge wetland interior, farm country inland, a panhandle town in the north-west and forested high ground in the north. Vice City itself splits into a mainland grid and a barrier island joined by causeways, the same basic layout as real Miami.

    • How often is this map updated?

      Whenever official material adds or changes a region. New trailers, official site updates and press assets can promote an entry from implied to confirmed, or add a region outright. Changes are logged in the changelog at the bottom of this page.

    Map, region and leak terminology

    Leonida

    Evidence level

    A named neighbourhood inside Vice City, as distinct from a region, which is a state-level area of Leonida.
    Estimate band
    A low–high range used instead of a single number when no authoritative figure exists. The GTA 6 band is 90–170 km².
