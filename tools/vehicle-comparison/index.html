What this page is: and what it is not

A comparison of the numbers the game shows, not a road test. Every figure on this page belongs to exactly one of three data classes, and the three are never mixed in one row.

  • Two-source verified

    Two independent guides agree on the figure, always with the sources and dates beside it.

  • Reported: not verified

    One reputable guide says it, or it comes from a single transcription. Labelled as reported, never presented as confirmed.

  • Not published

    No source has published it, measured top speed in mph or km/h, a purchase price, braking distances, grip figures. Shown as “—”, never filled in.

Why the answers differ

Three different #1s are in circulation, because they measure different things.

By in-game Speed rating, the Truffade Thrax leads at 8.7 (GameSpot, Beebom, ShowGamer, 20–22 Nov 2026). By estimated mph, the Grotti Itali RSX is listed at 135 mph (ProGameGuides / GTA 6 Bible, 19 Nov 2026), an estimate, disclosed as one on the page, not a test. By the biggest tables' partial data, the Pegassi '96 Infernus leads at 7.1 (IGN's table, 80+ of 277 rows; GTABase's ranking, 19 Nov 2026), only because the faster supercars are not in those tables yet. The estimate-based and rating-based rankings cannot both be right about which car is fastest: the RSX's in-game Speed rating is only 7.5, well below the Thrax's 8.7.

Until a timed, comparable top-speed test is published, with a track, a stock-vs-upgraded note and a named source, the honest answer is the rating ranking, and that is what this page shows. A page that picks one of the three #1s and calls it “the fastest car in GTA 6” would be a fourth wrong answer.

There is no dealership

You do not buy a car in GTA 6. You register a stolen one.

There is no car dealership and no car-buying site on the phone in the chapters played so far, and Rockstar has not mentioned one. You keep a car by paying to register it, at Moses in Watkins Auto Parts from chapter 2 (the first registration is free), or at Pay 'n' Spray from early chapter 3. The WAiNK scan card lists Access, Security, Tracker, Sell Value and Cost to Register. Sell Value is what a fence pays for the car, and it varies with condition and damage, damage cuts every price.

Two money figures on this page are not register costs and are never listed as such. The Emperor Sultan's $1,600 is Moses's reward for seven delivery jobs, a special case, not a market register price. Wyman's $1,500 is what he asks for each classic-car rebuild after you scan the wrecks (Ultimate Edition). Both are named here so they are not mistaken for the register column.

Ratings, not miles per hour

Speed 8.7 does not mean 8.7 of anything measured.

“Speed” on this page is the game's own rating out of 10, read from the vehicle's in-game stat card, which only appears once you have broken into the car and started the engine. No braking distance, grip figure, lateral G, suspension or wet-weather number has been published anywhere, and no datamine of vehicle handling files exists: the game is console-only with no PC release. A rating bar compares the numbers the game shows; it does not predict a race. Treat a Speed 8.7 supercar and a Speed 3.1 off-roader as what they are: opposite ends of the same ten-point scale, not a lap-time prediction.

The mph figures on some sites are estimates, GTA 5 carry-overs, or unsourced. One page claims an Emerus at 126.75 mph with a lap time and no test methodology, track or video named. Another reports a Thrax at 123.75 mph “once fully upgraded” with no source named. A third lists a Wastelander top speed of 91 mph and a lap time, explicitly GTA 5 data. None of these is cited or used anywhere on this page.

GTA 6 vehicle questions

The fastest car, the missing top speed, and where the numbers come from.

  • What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

    There is no measured answer yet. No outlet has published a timed, comparable top-speed test, so no mph figure exists for any vehicle. The highest in-game Speed rating among vehicles with published stats is the Truffade Thrax at 8.7 (GameSpot, Beebom, ShowGamer, 20–22 Nov 2026), but IGN's and GTABase's tables are still incomplete, so that is a rating ranking, not a measured fastest-car verdict. This page shows the ratings; it does not crown a fastest car.

  • What is the top speed of a GTA 6 car in mph?

    No measured top speed in mph or km/h exists for any GTA 6 vehicle. The guides that print mph figures label them estimates (ProGameGuides / GTA 6 Bible: Grotti Itali RSX est. 135 mph) or carry GTA 5 data by mistake. The game shows five ratings out of 10 per vehicle, Speed, Acceleration, Asphalt Handling, Off-Road Handling, Strength, and this tool compares those ratings. When a timed test is published, the measured figure will be added with its source and date.

  • How much does a car cost in GTA 6?

    There is no dealership and no purchase price. You keep a stolen car by paying to register it, Cost to Register, at Moses in Watkins Auto Parts from chapter 2 (the first registration is free) or at Pay 'n' Spray from early chapter 3. Sell Value is what a fence pays for the car, and it varies with condition and damage. The two figures are shown as separate columns on this page; there is no single “price”.

  • How many vehicles are in GTA 6?

    277 base models, per IGN's and PowerPyx's transcriptions of the in-game Progress menu (157 cars, 29 bikes, 28 watercraft, 16 aircraft and 47 service vehicles), plus around 280 Standout variants, one-off customised versions that do not count toward 100% (IGN counts 281, PowerPyx 280, GTABase 271, the three transcriptions disagree). This tool compares 115 vehicles with published stats, as of 24 Nov 2026. Standouts are excluded: whether they share their base model's ratings is not published.

  • Why do different sites name different fastest cars?

    Three different #1s are in circulation because they measure different things. By in-game Speed rating, the Truffade Thrax leads at 8.7. By estimated mph, the Grotti Itali RSX is listed at 135 mph, an estimate, not a test. By the biggest tables' partial data, the Pegassi '96 Infernus leads at 7.1, only because the faster supercars are not in those tables yet. Until a timed test exists, the honest answer is the rating ranking, and this page shows it.

  • How do I keep a car I steal in GTA 6?

    Scan it with the WAiNK phone app, then register it: at Moses in Watkins Auto Parts from chapter 2 (the first registration is free), or at Pay 'n' Spray from early chapter 3. The WAiNK scan card lists Access, Security, Tracker, Sell Value and Cost to Register. Unregistered cars are not kept.

  • Why do some rows show “—” for price?

    No price has been published for that vehicle yet, most vehicles have no published sell value or register cost, and the guides' tables are still growing. A “—” means not published, not zero and not free. Prices also vary with condition and damage, so the figures shown are as scanned. Single-source prices carry a * flag: one source, not yet confirmed by a second.

  • Where do the numbers on this page come from?

    From the game's own per-vehicle stat cards, as transcribed by IGN's Vehicles guide, Dexerto's wiki, Leonipedia / PlayDay.One and GameSpot, with prices from Sportskeeda, Gurugamer, EndgameTools and HowToVideoGame's WAiNK scans. Every figure carries a provenance badge; single-source figures are flagged provisional. Rockstar has published no performance stats, top speeds or prices itself. Stats may change with patches; the page is re-checked when the dataset grows.
