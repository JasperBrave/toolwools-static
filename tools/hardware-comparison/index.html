  2. 02

    Set resolution and workload

    1440p is the default. Workload matters: GTA Online applies a 1.0x CPU multiplier versus 0.70x for Story Mode, which alone can flip a balanced rig into a CPU bottleneck.

  3. 03

    Under 7% is functionally balanced, you will not feel it. 7-15% is noticeable in CPU-heavy scenes but fine overall. 15%+ means one part is dragging the other down and an upgrade will unlock real frame rate.

Four rules for reading a hardware comparison

The numbers above are only useful if you weight them correctly.

  • Average FPS sells cards; 1% lows decide how it feels

    Two GPUs within 5 frames of each other on average can feel completely different in a dense open world. When the spread between average and 1% lows exceeds 2:1, the bottleneck is CPU or VRAM, not raw GPU power.

  • Cost per frame only counts at your resolution

    A card that wins on cost per frame at 1080p can lose badly at 4K, where VRAM capacity and memory bandwidth start gating everything. Always compare within the resolution you actually play at.

  • VRAM is a cliff, not a slope

    Below your budget, capacity is irrelevant. One gigabyte over it, textures stream across PCIe and frame times spike. This is why a 16 GB mid-range card can outlast a faster 8 GB card in a game the size of Leonida.

  • Power draw is a system cost

    A 360 W card frequently needs a PSU upgrade and better case airflow. Fold both into the comparison before you decide one GPU is only $150 more than another.

Inside the comparison model

Cost per frame is street price divided by projected 1440p High average frame rate. The bottleneck verdict comes from the shared model: each part gets a 0-100 score, demand is scaled by resolution and workload, and the gap between GPU and CPU headroom decides the verdict and the FPS band.

costPerFrame = streetPrice / projected1440pHighAvgFps

headGpu = gpuScore / (100 x RES_FACTOR[res])       // 1080p 0.75, 1440p 0.90, 4K 1.00
headCpu = cpuScore / (100 x WORKLOAD_FACTOR[mode]) // story 0.70, online 1.00, stream 1.15
delta   = headGpu - headCpu
verdict = delta > 0.07 ? CPU bottleneck : delta < -0.07 ? GPU bottleneck : balanced
avgFps  = min(headGpu, headCpu) x BASE_FPS[res]    // 1080p 130, 1440p 100, 4K 65
band    = [avgFps x 0.8, avgFps x 1.15]
  • GPU and CPU scores use the shared 34-GPU, 21-CPU catalog, sorted by score. RTX 4090 = 100, Core i9-14900K = 100. The catalog includes the RTX 5060, RTX 5060 Ti, RX 9070 series and Intel Arc B580.
  • Workload multipliers: GTA Online 1.00x CPU demand versus 0.70x for Story Mode, which alone can flip a balanced rig into a CPU bottleneck. Streaming adds 1.15x.
  • The upgrade suggestion is the smallest part, by score, that returns the rig to balanced at the same resolution and workload.
  • Projected GPU table figures are copied from the source model; the live comparison uses the shared engine catalog, so its numbers may differ slightly from the table.

GPU and CPU questions before you buy

Straight answers on capacity, cost per frame and upgrade timing.

  • Which GPU is best for GTA 6?

    For 1440p High at a locked 60 FPS with room to spare, the RX 9070 XT and RTX 5070 Ti are the value picks, both carry 16 GB of VRAM, which matters more than raw shader throughput in a world this large. The RTX 5080 is the safe 4K choice. Below $300, the RTX 3060 12 GB remains the most sensible entry card because of its capacity, not its speed.

  • Is an 8 GB graphics card enough for GTA 6?

    For 1080p Medium with an upscaler, probably. For 1440p High, no. GTA 6 is expected to push texture budgets past 8 GB at High settings, and once you exceed capacity the penalty lands on 1% lows as visible stutter rather than on the average frame rate.

  • Does CPU matter for GTA 6 or is it GPU-bound?

    Both, at different resolutions. At 4K the GPU dominates and almost any modern 6-core keeps up. At 1080p with a fast card, GTA 6's traffic and pedestrian simulation is CPU-heavy enough that an older 6-core will cap your frame rate well below what the GPU could deliver.

  • How do you calculate cost per frame?

    Current street price divided by projected 1440p High average frame rate. It is a blunt instrument, it ignores VRAM headroom, power draw and resale value, so use it to shortlist two or three cards, then compare those on the factors in the reading guide above.

  • Are these GTA 6 benchmark numbers official?

    No. Rockstar has published no PC benchmarks. Every figure here comes from our own model, calibrated against RDR2 and GTA V PC on the same RAGE engine and cross-checked with published third-party benchmark suites. We replace projections with measured results within 48 hours of the PC launch.

  • Should I upgrade my GPU now or wait for GTA 6?

    Wait if your current card clears the recommended tier, pre-launch pricing on the tiers that matter has historically softened once real benchmarks land. Upgrade now only if you are below the minimum spec or stuck on 8 GB and playing at 1440p, where you would be buying the upgrade regardless of GTA 6.
