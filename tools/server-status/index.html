The three services GTA 6 depends on

One card each, same shape: what it is, what breaks when it is down, the official page, and the last hand check.

  • Rockstar Authentication

    Status: Operational

    When it is down: Rockstar account sign-in fails. It only matters if the game asks for it, whether GTA 6 needs a Rockstar sign-in at first launch is unknown; Rockstar has not said.

  • PlayStation Network

    Status: Operational

    Sony’s status page for PlayStation Network: sign-in, PlayStation Store, downloads and the licence check.

    When it is down: sign-in fails, the Store and downloads stop, and a digital copy may not start, it checks its licence with PSN when it opens.

    Check the official page →

    All services are up and running on Sony’s status page. Last checked 27 November 2026, 07:30 UTC.

  • Xbox network

    Status: Operational

    Microsoft’s status page for the Xbox network: sign-in, Microsoft Store, downloads and the licence check.

    When it is down: sign-in fails, the Store and downloads stop, and a digital copy may not start on a non-Home Xbox, it checks its licence with the Xbox network when it opens.

What breaks what

GTA 6 itself has no servers. These are the things that can actually stop you playing, and the things that cannot.

What breaks when each service is down, and what does not affect GTA 6.
If this is downWhat breaks for GTA 6
PlayStation NetworkA digital copy may not start, it checks its licence with PSN when it opens. Downloads stop, and the PlayStation Store is unavailable.
Xbox networkThe same on Xbox: a digital copy may not start on a non-Home Xbox, downloads stop, and the Microsoft Store is unavailable.
Rockstar AuthenticationRockstar account sign-in fails. It only matters if the game asks for it, whether GTA 6 needs a Rockstar sign-in at first launch is unknown; Rockstar has not said.
Patch and download serversTitle-update downloads stop. Patches run on the platform servers, not on Rockstar game servers, there are no GTA 6 game servers to download from.
PS5 system software 14.23PS5 system software 14.23 (1.267 GB, released 17 Nov 2026) is mandatory, GTA 6 will not run until the console is updated to it.
Story mode itselfNothing. Once installed, story mode runs offline on the console, no service needs to be up for you to keep playing.

Does NOT affect GTA 6: GTA Online, Red Dead Online and FiveM. An outage in any of those does not stop GTA 6, they are separate online services, and GTA 6 has no online mode.

GTA 6 server status questions

Whether GTA 6 is down, what can actually stop it starting, and how this page is checked.

  • Is GTA 6 down?

    No, GTA 6 has no servers, so it cannot be “down”. It launched 19 Nov 2026 as a single-player game with no online mode. If the game will not start, one of the three services above is the cause: Rockstar Authentication, PlayStation Network or the Xbox network. Their status is checked by hand, and each row links to the official page.

  • Does GTA 6 have online servers?

    No. GTA 6 launched as a single-player experience with no online mode, and Rockstar has not announced one. Rockstar’s own status page has no GTA VI row. There is no GTA 6 server status to show, only the status of the services the game depends on.

  • Why won’t GTA 6 start?

    Usually a licence check or a service outage, not the game itself. A digital copy checks its licence with PlayStation Network or the Xbox network when it starts, so if that service is down the game may not open. Check the three status rows above, then restart the console and check the licence. Once installed, story mode itself runs offline. Whether the game asks for a Rockstar account sign-in at first launch is not confirmed, Rockstar has not said.

  • Is the PSN outage a GTA 6 outage?

    No. The three outages in the timeline above were PlayStation Network outages, Gaming and Social degraded, with sign-in and Store problems, that stopped players signing in or starting a digital copy of GTA 6. There is no GTA 6 server to be down, so no outage can be a “GTA 6 outage”.

  • How often is this page checked?

    By hand. The three official status pages were last checked 27 November 2026, 07:30 UTC, that stamp is on every row. A status that has not been re-checked in 7 days shows “check stale, see official page” instead of its old value, so a stale check is never shown as current. New incidents are added to the timeline by hand when a service owner acknowledges them.

  • Why did the Xbox row say “check unavailable”?

    Our first-hand checks of Microsoft’s Xbox status page on 22 and 24 Nov 2026 returned only a loading shell, the page is JavaScript-rendered and no service data could be read, so the row showed “check unavailable, see official page” rather than green: a status we could not read must not be presented as healthy. On 26 Nov 2026 we verified the page in a live browser: it reads “All services up and running”, all 11 services normal.

  • Can I trust Downdetector reports for GTA 6?

    Only as user reports, never as an outage. Downdetector has a Rockstar Games page, but no GTA 6 page, GTA 6 has no servers to report on. A spike in user reports measures player worry, not a confirmed failure, and Downdetector’s terms forbid republishing its data, so this page links to it and does not reproduce it.

  • What is the latest GTA 6 patch?

    01.002.002, released 23 Nov 2026, the first post-launch patch, 603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, with seven fixes from Rockstar Support including crashes, autosave and the Wanted Level. Before it: 01.002.000 (13 Nov) and 01.002.001 (16 Nov), both reported with no Rockstar notes. No newer update, no 01.002.003, exists as of 24 Nov 2026.
