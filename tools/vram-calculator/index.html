How much VRAM GTA 6 needs, setting by setting

Every toggle below has a measurable memory cost.

  1. 01

    Pick your resolution

    1080p, 1440p or 4K. Framebuffers, G-buffer and post-processing chains scale with pixels — 4K needs ~1.8 GB more just to render a frame.

  2. 02

    Pick a quality preset

    Low → Ultra maps to a texture-pool size. Ultra uses uncompressed streaming and is the single biggest VRAM lever.

  3. 03

    Toggle ray tracing, frame gen and HD textures

    Each adds a separate overhead. Ray tracing costs 0.8–1.9 GB depending on resolution. Frame generation adds 0.4–1.1 GB. The HD pack can add up to 2.2 GB.

The VRAM budget model, line by line

Every preset carries a base texture-pool size in GB. Base scales with resolution (1080p × 1.0, 1440p × 1.28, 4K × 1.72) to account for framebuffers and G-buffer. Ray tracing, frame generation and the HD texture pack are separate additive overheads. Recommended VRAM = raw total + a per-resolution headroom band (1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 GB) that maps to the driver's spillover threshold in RDR2 / Cyberpunk 2077 measurements.

base          = BASE[preset] × RES_MULT[res]          // GB
overhead      = rt ? RT[res] : 0
              + frameGen ? FG[res] : 0
              + hdTextures ? HDT[preset] : 0

totalGB       = round1(base + overhead)
recommendedGB = roundHalf(totalGB + HEADROOM[res])

verdict:
  card ≥ recommendedGB → Comfortable
  card ≥ totalGB       → Adequate (no reserve)
  card ≥ totalGB − H   → Tight   (driver fallback zone)
  otherwise            → Insufficient (spillover to system RAM)
  • Base texture-pool sizes are calibrated from RDR2 and Cyberpunk 2077 telemetry at each preset.
  • Resolution multipliers (1080p × 1.00, 1440p × 1.28, 4K × 1.72) match pixel-scaled framebuffer and G-buffer measurements.
  • Ray-tracing overhead is derived from Cyberpunk 2077 RT-on-vs-off VRAM deltas at matched settings.
  • HD-texture-pack overhead scales with preset because higher presets already use larger mip levels, leaving less to swap in.
  • Recommended VRAM adds a per-resolution headroom band (1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 GB) that maps to the driver's spillover threshold before texture pop starts.
  • Every card in the picker uses its manufacturer-spec VRAM in GB. VRAM-variant cards (3060 12/8 GB, 4060 Ti 16/8 GB, 5060 Ti 16/8 GB) are separate entries.

GTA 6 VRAM questions

Is 8 GB enough, does RT cost memory, what happens when you run out.

  • At 1080p High, 8 GB is comfortable. At 1440p Ultra, 12 GB is the clean floor. At 4K Ultra with ray tracing, plan for 18 GB. The HD texture pack adds another 1–2 GB on top of any preset. Pick your resolution and preset above for the exact number.

  • Yes for 1080p High and 1440p High. Insufficient at 1440p Very High. Not enough for 1440p Ultra with ray tracing, 4K Ultra, or the HD texture pack at High and above. Stay at 1080p and skip the HD pack and an 8 GB RTX 4060 / RX 7600 will land in the Comfortable zone.

  • Only at 1080p Low or Medium without the HD texture pack. 6 GB (GTX 1660 Super, RTX 2060, RX 5600 XT) is below the 1080p High recommended buffer, and every other resolution / preset combination will fall into the Tight or Insufficient zone.

  • Yes at 1440p Ultra, and at 4K High without the HD pack. Add ray tracing at 1440p Ultra and 12 GB drops to Tight. 12 GB (RTX 4070 / 4070 Super / RTX 3060 12GB / RX 6700 XT) is the recommended launch-window minimum if you play at 1440p.

  • Tight at 4K Ultra (16.2 GB needed of 16 GB), and Insufficient at 4K Ultra with ray tracing (18.1 GB). Add the HD texture pack and frame generation and it stays Insufficient — a 24 GB 4090 is the only card that stays Comfortable in that stack.

  • Ray tracing adds ~0.8 GB at 1080p, ~1.2 GB at 1440p, and ~1.9 GB at 4K on top of the base preset. Path tracing (if Rockstar ships it) will add roughly 2× that. If your card is already Tight without RT, enabling RT will push it into Insufficient.

  • Upscaling shrinks the internal render resolution, which reduces framebuffer and G-buffer VRAM by 0.4–1.8 GB depending on mode and target resolution. Frame generation (DLSS 3 FG, FSR 3 FG) adds its own buffer — 0.4 GB at 1080p, 1.1 GB at 4K — so it partly offsets the saving.

  • The HD pack adds an uncompressed texture layer on top of the preset's base pool. Overhead scales with preset: 0 GB at Low, ~0.4 GB at Medium, ~0.8 GB at High, ~1.4 GB at Very High, and ~2.2 GB at Ultra. Skip the HD pack if your card is under 12 GB.

Rockstar's Vice City rebuild is being authored at 4K asset resolution with higher-density streaming, so VRAM pressure at Ultra scales with texture density, not just pixels.

  • 4K High is adequate on 12 GB, tight on 10 GB. 4K Ultra needs 16.2 GB, so 16 GB is tight and 20 GB is comfortable. 4K Ultra + ray tracing needs 18.1 GB. 4K Ultra + RT + HD pack + frame generation needs 20+ GB — that's 4090 / 7900 XTX territory.

  • For raw rasterisation, the 3070 is faster. For VRAM headroom at 1440p Ultra or with the HD texture pack, the 3060 12 GB meets the total pool while the 3070 8 GB lands in the Insufficient zone. If you run 1440p with textures maxed, the 3060 12 GB is the safer pick.

  • Close enough to plan a purchase, not close enough to quote. Base pool sizes are calibrated from RDR2 (same RAGE engine), Cyberpunk 2077 texture-pool telemetry, and modded GTA V HD pack allocation. Ray-tracing overheads match measured Cyberpunk RT / Path Tracing deltas. Expect ±10% versus launch-day drivers. We refresh within hours of Rockstar's official spec drop.

  • Yes — that's the exact failure mode of the Insufficient verdict. When VRAM overflows, the driver streams textures from system RAM over PCIe, and you get pop-in, texture LOD flicker and 1% low frame time spikes. Average FPS often looks fine; the felt experience is stuttery. Drop preset or resolution to bring VRAM inside the pool.

  • Streaming uses less VRAM but more disk I/O. GTA 6 targets NVMe with DirectStorage; a slow SATA SSD (or worse, an HDD) forces the engine to keep a larger resident pool in VRAM to hide load stalls. On DirectStorage-friendly NVMe drives the calculator's numbers hold; on SATA SSDs plan for 10–15% more headroom.

VRAM and texture streaming terms

VRAM
Video RAM — dedicated memory on your GPU that holds textures, framebuffers, and render targets. Distinct from system RAM.
Texture pool
The pre-allocated VRAM region the engine reserves for texture streaming. Preset scales this pool directly — Ultra roughly doubles Low.
Framebuffer
The image being rendered plus depth and G-buffer targets. Scales with pixels: ~1 GB at 1080p, ~1.7 GB at 1440p, ~3.5 GB at 4K.
Ray tracing overhead
Extra VRAM used by BVH structures, ray payloads and denoiser targets. Adds 0.8–1.9 GB depending on resolution.
Frame generation
DLSS 3 FG and FSR 3 FG insert a synthetic frame between real ones, buffering optical-flow data in VRAM. Adds 0.4–1.1 GB.
DirectStorage
GPU-decompressed asset streaming from NVMe. Reduces required resident texture pool by streaming just-in-time.
