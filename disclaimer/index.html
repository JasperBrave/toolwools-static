Estimates are projections, not facts

Rockstar Games has not published PC system requirements for GTA 6, and has not announced a PC release date. Every system requirement, frame-rate estimate, VRAM figure, tier score and price conversion on this site is a projection derived from the announced console targets and from measured behaviour in comparable games. Projections carry stated error bands and named sources, and they will change as Rockstar announces more. Do not treat them as specifications.

Dates

The only launch date on this site is the one Rockstar Games announced: November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the game launched on that date. Any other date on this site is either a Rockstar announcement with its source attached, or our own dated analysis, labelled as such. Pre-order and preload timing are now history, and there is no PC version: Rockstar has not announced a PC release date.

Not buying advice

Tier scores, bottleneck verdicts and readiness scores are estimates to help you plan, not purchase orders. A score does not tell you to buy a card, and a ranking does not tell you where to buy a game. Prices shown are conversions at a stated rate date, not offers, and they change with exchange rates and tax.

Accuracy and corrections

We work to keep every number accurate and we publish the method behind each one on the methodology page, so any claim can be checked. If a number is wrong, report it to corrections@toolwools.com with your setup and your measurement. We reproduce it on the reference rig and credit the reporter.
