Email questions, answered

What we send, how often, and how to stop it.

  • What emails do you send?

    Four kinds, and only the kinds you pick: tool launches and betas, patch-day guides, subscriber-only drops, and major GTA 6 news. Each one is listed on this page with what it contains.

  • How often will you email me?

    Weekly, monthly, or only on major news — your choice. Weekly is the default; major-news-only means you hear from us only when Rockstar announces something.

  • How do I stop all emails?

    Use the one-click opt-out on this page with the address you subscribed with, or reply to any email with the word unsubscribe. Either way, you are removed immediately and we will not email you again.

  • Do you sell my email address?

    No. Your address is used only to send the emails you asked for. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

  • I lost my manage link. What now?

    Use the one-click opt-out below with the address you subscribed with, or email hello@toolwools.com from that address and we will update your preferences by hand.
