Contact

Talk to the people behind the tools

Report a bug, correct a number, suggest a tool, or propose a partnership. Every message goes to the editorial team — no bots, no ticket numbers.

Where your message goes

Four queues, one team. Pick the route that matches your message and it lands with the right person first.

Bug report

Something on the site behaves wrongly — a broken calculation, a dead link, a layout fault. Attach a screenshot or the exact inputs if you can.

Data correction

A tier, price, spec or score that does not match your rig. Give us the tool, the value we gave, the value you measured, and how you measured it.

Tool feedback

Tell us what a tool gets wrong, what it should do next, or what to build. The feedback goes to the same queue the roadmap is drawn from.

Partnership

Business, press, or distribution proposals. We answer partnership mail the same way we answer the rest: in writing, with a name attached.

What happens next

  1. Receipt. You see the confirmation on this page the moment the message lands in our inbox.
  2. Triage. Bugs and data corrections go first, usually within two working days; other mail follows in order.
  3. Reproduction. For a data correction, we re-run the case on the reference rig before anything changes.
  4. Reply. We answer in writing, to the address you gave. If a fix ships, it is dated in the tool's changelog.

Prefer email? Write to hello@toolwools.com, or corrections@toolwools.com for wrong numbers.

Before you send

Response times, what to include, and what happens to your message.

  • How fast will I hear back?

    Bugs and data corrections are triaged first, usually within two working days. General feedback and partnership mail is answered in writing, in the order it arrives.

  • What should I include in a bug report?

    The tool and page, the exact inputs you used, what happened, and what you expected. A screenshot of the result helps, and a browser name and version helps more.

  • Do I need an account to contact you?

    No. There are no accounts on this site. The form asks for an email so we can reply to you; that address is used only to answer this message.

  • What happens to my message?

    It is stored in our contact inbox and read by the editorial team. It is not sold, shared, or added to any mailing list. See the privacy policy for the full retention detail.

  • Can I report a wrong number here?

    Yes — choose Data correction, and include the tool, the value we gave, the value you measured, and how you measured it. Corrections are reproduced on the reference rig before a fix ships.
