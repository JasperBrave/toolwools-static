GTA 6 news, sourced

Six things that actually happened, with the source attached to each one. Rockstar's own announcements first, then our dated analysis of what is still unconfirmed.

Rockstar

GTA 6 is out now on PS5 and Xbox

November 19, 2026

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The install is up to 212 GB, and the game runs at 30 FPS on all consoles, with no performance mode.

Source: Rockstar Newsroom

Rockstar

GTA 6 launch pushed to November 19, 2026

May 2, 2025

Rockstar Games confirms a targeted release date of November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. PC release will follow.

Source: Rockstar Newsroom

Rockstar

Trailer 2 reveals Jason, Lucia and a reborn Vice City

May 6, 2025

The second official trailer expands on the Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic between Jason and Lucia, showcasing Leonida, the Everglades and a densely populated Vice City.

Source: Rockstar Games

Rockstar

Trailer 1: welcome to Vice City

Dec 5, 2023

Rockstar's first official reveal confirmed the Leonida setting, the dual-protagonist premise and a 2025 window that has since shifted.

Source: Rockstar Games

Analysis

Pre-orders expected to open a few months before launch

Q3 2026

Rockstar has historically opened pre-orders roughly 3-4 months ahead of release. Editions, bonuses and pricing tiers have not been confirmed.

Update — Nov 20, 2026: Pre-orders opened in 2026, and GTA 6 launched Nov 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The confirmed lineup is Standard ($79.99) and Ultimate ($99.99), digital-only.

Source: ToolWools analysis

Analysis

PC version: no dated window yet

Ongoing

Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. Following Rockstar's pattern with GTA V and RDR2, expect a gap of 6-18 months after the console launch.

Source: ToolWools analysis
