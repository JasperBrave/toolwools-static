About

Who builds ToolWools

ToolWools is an independent editorial site for GTA 6 tools, calculators, trackers, and planners. Every number on the site is modelled, benchmarked, and reviewed by named people — and every change is dated in the tool's changelog.

Editorial disclosure

ToolWools is reader-supported but independently operated. Our calculators, tier scores, and verdicts are not sponsored by Rockstar Games, NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, or any hardware vendor. We accept no payment, free hardware, or affiliate placement in exchange for scores. Every part is measured against the same public methodology and re-benchmarked when new drivers or engine builds ship.

What this site is, and what it isn't

Worth stating plainly, because the GTA 6 information space is full of pages that blur the line between an estimate and an announcement.

What ToolWools is

  • A free set of GTA 6 calculators, checkers and planners that run entirely in your browser.
  • An estimate-first site: every number carries a stated error band and a dated source list.
  • Independently operated and reader-supported, with no sponsored scores and no paid tier placement.
  • Maintained continuously — models are re-anchored on every driver cycle and at launch, not written once.

What it isn't

  • Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
  • Not a source of official system requirements — Rockstar has not published PC specs yet.
  • Not a leaks site. Unverified material may be discussed and labelled, but it never moves a calculation.
  • Not an account-gated product. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay for.

How this site makes money

A short section, because the answer is short — and because you should not have to guess whether a recommendation was bought.

Reader-supported, ad-light

The site is funded by display placement and, in future, optional reader support. Whatever runs alongside the content has no input into it: nobody buying space sees a score before it publishes.

No paid placement, ever

We accept no payment, no loaned or gifted hardware, and no affiliate arrangement in exchange for a tier, a verdict, or a recommendation. If that ever changes, it will be disclosed on this page before it changes anywhere else.

Recommendations are not monetised differently

A card placed in S tier and a card placed in D tier earn this site exactly the same amount, which is nothing. The tier formula is published on the methodology page so you can check the placement yourself.

Your data, and what we don't do with it

  • Every calculator runs client-side in your browser. Your part list, resolution and settings are never sent to a server.
  • There are no accounts, no logins, and no email requirement to use any tool on the site.
  • Shareable result links encode your inputs in the URL itself, which is why they work without us storing anything.
  • Saved results, where a tool offers them, live in your own browser storage and are cleared when you clear site data.
  • We use aggregate analytics only after you accept it in the consent banner. No profile is built and nothing is sold. Tool inputs never leave your device. Read the full policy in our privacy policy.

About this site, answered

Ownership, independence, data handling and corrections in short form.

  • Who runs this site?

    A small independent editorial team: a hardware desk that builds and benchmarks the models, a panel of external benchmark reviewers who cross-check them, and an editor who owns sourcing and corrections. All three are described by name and remit on this page, and each tool's byline links back here.

  • Are these the official GTA 6 system requirements?

    No. Rockstar Games has not published PC system requirements for GTA 6. Every tier, score and frame-rate band on this site is an estimate derived from the announced console targets and from measured behaviour in comparable RAGE-engine titles. Estimates are labelled as estimates on every page that shows one.

  • How do I report a number that looks wrong?

    Use the corrections address on this page and include the tool, your exact inputs, the value we gave, the value you measured, and how you measured it. We reproduce it on the reference rig first — reproduction is the gate — then ship a dated fix in the tool's changelog and credit you unless you ask us not to.

  • Do you accept sponsored placement or free hardware?

    No. No vendor pays for a tier, a verdict, or a mention, and we do not accept loaned or gifted parts in exchange for coverage. Every card and CPU goes through the same published formula, and the formula itself is on the methodology page so any placement can be checked independently.

  • Do you store my hardware details?

    No. Calculations run in your browser and your inputs never reach a server. Share links encode inputs in the URL, and any saved results live in your own browser storage. There are no accounts on this site. Analytics is aggregate only, and only runs after you accept it in the consent banner.
