Privacy Policy

What ToolWools collects and why: tool inputs never leave your device, analytics runs only with your consent, and your email is never sold.

Summary

ToolWools collects as little as possible. Tool inputs never leave your device. Analytics runs only after you accept it in the consent banner, and only in aggregate. Your email address is collected only when you give it to us, to subscribe to the newsletter or to send a message, and it is never sold or shared. This page is the full policy.

Last updated November 17, 2026.

What we collect, and why

What we do not collect

  • Tool inputs. Every calculator runs in your browser. Your GPU, CPU, resolution and settings never reach a server.
  • Accounts. There are no accounts on this site, and nothing to sign up for to use a tool.
  • Payment details. There is nothing to buy on this site.

What we do collect

  • Email address, when you give it. Subscribing to the newsletter, or sending a contact message, stores the address you typed and the message you wrote, in our Supabase database, so we can send the emails you asked for and answer you. That is the only use.
  • Aggregate analytics, only with your consent. Google Analytics (GA4, measurement ID G-FXX0Z11D8D) loads only after you accept it in the consent banner. It records aggregate page views and device categories, with IP anonymisation on. No profile is built, no individual is identified, and nothing is sold.
  • Your consent choice. Stored in your own browser (localStorage) so the banner does not ask again on every visit. You can change it any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
  • Saved results, in your browser. Where a tool offers to save a result, it is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Who we share it with

Nobody buys your data. Email addresses are held by our email database provider (Supabase) solely to store and deliver the mail you asked for, under their data-processing terms. Analytics data is held by Google under its own terms, only when you have consented. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe, at which point the address is marked unsubscribed and no further mail is sent. Contact messages are kept as long as needed to answer them and to maintain the corrections record, then deleted. Analytics data is retained by Google for 14 months, the shortest retention Google Analytics offers, and only for visitors who consented.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@toolwools.com from the address in question. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time from the link in any email, or from the email preferences page. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer; withdrawing consent stops further analytics, it does not delete data already collected in aggregate.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no tracking cookies of its own. Your theme preference and your consent choice are stored in localStorage in your browser. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set its own cookies under Google's terms; if you decline, it loads nothing and sets nothing. The YouTube embeds on the trailers page load from youtube-nocookie.com and set no cookies until you play a video.

Questions

Write to hello@toolwools.com. We answer privacy questions the same way we answer everything else: in writing, with a name attached.
