GTA 6 trailers, both of them

Two official trailers so far. Watch either one here, or read the beat-by-beat breakdown first.

Trailer 1

December 5, 2023 · 1:31

The first reveal. Vice City's neon at dusk, the return of Leonida, and the first look at Lucia and Jason. It establishes the Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic in under ninety seconds and ends on the city skyline.

What happens in Trailer 1

  • Vice City skyline at dusk, neon and rain
  • Lucia in the prison yard, the first voice heard
  • Jason on a beach at dawn, the partner introduced
  • The title card: Grand Theft Auto VI, Leonida

Trailer 2

May 6, 2025 · 3:22

The full story trailer. Jason and Lucia's life in Leonida, the Everglades, the Keys, and the scale of Vice City — the densest world Rockstar has built. It confirms the November 19, 2026 date at the end.

What happens in Trailer 2

  • Jason and Lucia driving the Everglades at dawn
  • Vice City at night, the densest crowd Rockstar has shown
  • The Keys, the swamp, and Leonida's open country
  • The closing card: November 19, 2026

Trailer questions, answered

Where to watch, whether a third trailer exists, and what the end card confirms.

  • Where can I watch the trailers?

    On Rockstar's official YouTube channel, and right here: the Watch button on each card opens the trailer in a dialog that plays the official upload.

  • Is there a third trailer?

    Not yet. Rockstar has released two official trailers so far. If a third is announced, it will appear on this page with its date and source.

  • What is the release date shown at the end of Trailer 2?

    November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The PC version has no dated window yet.

  • Are the locations in the trailers real places?

    The setting is Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida. Vice City is the fictional Miami, and the trailers show the Everglades, the Keys and the surrounding countryside.

  • Can I download the trailers?

    We link to Rockstar's official uploads rather than hosting copies. Use the Watch button on each card, or find them on Rockstar's YouTube channel.
