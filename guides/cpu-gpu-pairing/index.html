CPU-GPU pairing for GTA 6

Twelve real pairings, labelled balanced, CPU-bound or GPU-starved — and the reason draw calls, not raw power, decide which one yours is.

In short

Balance is decided by draw calls, not raw power. At 1080p the CPU is often the limiter; at 4K the GPU almost always is. The twelve pairings below are labelled balanced, CPU-bound or GPU-starved at the resolution they were tested at — check yours at the resolution you actually play.

Why draw calls decide the balance

An open world is thousands of objects per frame, and each one is a draw call the CPU must issue before the GPU can render it. A CPU that issues calls slowly caps the frame rate no matter how fast the GPU is, because the GPU spends its time waiting. This is why single-thread pace matters as much as core count, and why the bottleneck model weighs draw-call throughput above raw clock speed.

The practical consequence: upgrading the GPU on a CPU-bound system buys nothing until the CPU is fixed, and the cheapest fix is usually a CPU one tier up, not a GPU two tiers up. The Bottleneck Calculator shows the smallest upgrade that actually moves the number.

Pairing questions, answered

What CPU-bound means, why draw calls matter, and how resolution shifts the balance.

  • What does CPU-bound actually mean?

    It means the CPU cannot issue draw calls fast enough for the GPU to stay busy. The GPU waits, frames drop, and a stronger card changes nothing until the CPU is fixed. At 1080p this is common; at 4K it is rare.

  • Why do draw calls matter more than raw power?

    An open world is thousands of objects per frame, each one a draw call the CPU must issue. A CPU that issues calls slowly caps the frame rate no matter how fast the GPU renders. This is why single-thread pace and thread count both appear in the bottleneck model.

  • Is a balanced pairing always the best buy?

    For the money, usually yes — a balanced pairing spends the budget where it buys frames. An unbalanced pairing can still be right if you plan to upgrade one part soon, or if you play at a resolution where the balance shifts.

  • Does the balance change with resolution?

    Yes, and it changes a lot. At 1080p the CPU is often the limiter; at 4K the GPU almost always is. A pairing labelled CPU-bound at 1080p can be balanced at 1440p and GPU-starved at 4K. Check the pairing at the resolution you actually play.

  • How do I check my own pairing?

    Use the Bottleneck Calculator with your exact CPU, GPU, resolution and frame-rate target. It returns a score, a verdict, and the smallest upgrade that fixes the imbalance.
